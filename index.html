THE WORLD SHOULD BE CONCERNED

ON HAITI'S STRUGGLE

The world should care when Haiti suffers — not because we are different, but because we are the same. We are all children of God. A nation should not be forgotten because it struggles. Our future depends on compassion, unity, and understanding — not silence.

If the world continues to ignore Haiti, history will remember the silence—not the suffering. But if the world stands with Haiti, hope rises, and a better future becomes possible.
